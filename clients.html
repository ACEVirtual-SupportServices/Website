ACE Match

Talent Placement

When you need your own dedicated person. We run the search, assess for compatibility as well as capability, and present matched candidates. You contract with them directly.

  • Needs discovery → compatibility shortlist of 3 candidates
  • Typical placement timeline: three to four weeks
  • Matched beyond the CV: work style, industry fit, capacity
  • ACE Manage available as an optional ongoing layer
Sourcing and matching fee
30%
of the first month's agreed pay, minimum $100, quoted after your scope session
  • Half to begin the search, half when you accept a candidate, typically three to four weeks later
  • Hiring more than one? Additional roles are discounted
  • One free replacement within the first 30 days
  • Nothing further to pay after placement

For example, if the role pays $2,000 a month, the sourcing fee is $600.

ACE Manage

Operational Layer

When you want the person, not the managing. You still pay the sourcing fee to find them. From then on you receive one monthly invoice covering their work and our management, so there is one relationship to hold and one payment to make. Always added on top of ACE Match; never bought on its own.

  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Structured check-ins with your professional
  • Issue escalation and mediation
  • Continuity cover when they are unavailable
  • A replacement guarantee that never expires

How It Works

From first contact
to first working day.

Our placement process is structured, transparent, and built around finding the right fit, not the fastest one.

You reach out and complete our intake form. We gather your requirements, context, and operational needs before anything moves forward.

A 35-minute structured call with our founder. We get into the detail of how you work, your communication style, and what a successful placement looks like.

We search our curated database and shortlist three candidates matched on skills, personality, work style, and capacity, not just availability.

You choose your VSP. We brief them, confirm expectations on both sides, and either hand off cleanly or stay in as your ACE Manage layer.

A Note on Specific Needs

Some clients need
a different kind of support.

Running a business is rarely straightforward. Some of our clients manage health considerations, irregular schedules, or communication preferences that require a more structured operational layer than standard management provides.

Our High-Touch tier within ACE Manage was built for exactly this. High-Touch suitability is identified through our intake process, not self-declared.

High-Touch includes everything Standard does, plus:

  • Async-first communication structures, no forced real-time contact
  • Weekly check-ins rather than bi-weekly
  • Extended response windows built into the working agreement
  • Active buffer management between client and VSP
  • All escalation handled entirely by ACE-VSS

High-Touch availability is confirmed at the Founder Discovery Call stage.
