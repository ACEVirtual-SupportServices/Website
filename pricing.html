Step one

The scope session

$50

You know you need help. You are less sure what to hand over, or to whom. That is what the scope session is for.

In an hour we work out what should come off your plate, what a professional would own, and what kind of engagement actually fits. You leave with a written scope document, whether or not you work with us.

$50, credited in full against your first invoice if you start within 60 days. A free 15-minute fit call comes first.

Monthly support

ACE Micro

A vetted professional handles a defined set of deliverables each month. No search fee, no management charge. One invoice.

Starter

$260 to $310 / month

Around 10 hours of work a month. Where the price falls depends on the expertise the work needs.

  • For example: basic admin support, covering calendar, scheduling and file organisation
  • or 8 to 12 social posts, designed, captioned and scheduled
  • or 4 to 6 short-form video edits
  • or two blog posts

Steady

$470 to $580 / month

Around 20 hours of work a month. Where the price falls depends on the expertise the work needs.

  • For example: ongoing admin support with calendar and inbox oversight
  • or one platform managed end to end, with content calendar, 16 to 20 posts and community replies
  • or 10 to 12 short-form video edits, captioned and scheduled
  • or email marketing set up and a monthly newsletter produced

Embedded

$940 to $1,150 / month

Around 40 hours of work a month. Where the price falls depends on the expertise the work needs.

  • For example: a combined role such as admin plus social media
  • or a full content operation across two platforms
  • or email marketing strategy, segmentation and campaign production
  • or an ongoing video pipeline with repurposing across formats

Three-month minimum, invoiced monthly in advance. Need mid-level, senior or fractional support? Those engagements are quoted individually after your scope session rather than sold as packages.

Placement

ACE Match

When you need your own dedicated person. We run the search, assess for compatibility as well as capability, and present matched candidates. You contract with them and pay them directly.

Sourcing and matching fee
30%

of the first month’s agreed pay, minimum $100.

  • Half to begin the search, half when you accept a candidate, typically three to four weeks later
  • Hiring more than one? Additional roles are discounted
  • One free replacement within the first 30 days
  • Nothing further to pay after placement

For example, if the role pays $2,000 a month, the sourcing fee is $600.

Placement, managed

ACE Manage

When you want the person, not the managing. You still pay the sourcing fee to find them. From then on you receive one monthly invoice covering their work and our management, so there is one relationship to hold and one payment to make.

  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Structured check-ins with your professional
  • Issue escalation and mediation
  • Continuity cover when they are unavailable
  • A replacement guarantee that never expires

Quoted after your scope session. No percentage, no fixed figures published.

Our Commitment

Our fees are charged to you, never taken from your professional’s compensation. That is deliberate: people who are paid properly stay, and placements that last are the only ones worth making.
