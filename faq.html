Common questions

Before you ask

For businesses

What if it does not work out?

We replace them at no additional sourcing fee within the first 30 days. Under ACE Manage that guarantee stays live for the whole engagement.

How long does placement take?

Typically three to four weeks from your scope session to a shortlist.

Are they employees or contractors?

Independent professionals. You are not taking on an employment relationship.

How do time zones work?

Agreed at the scope session and written into the brief, so overlap is deliberate.

Who provides software and tools?

You do, or the choice is left to the professional. Tool costs are never deducted from their pay.

Can I hire more than one?

Yes. Additional roles in the same search are discounted.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes. Placements carry a three-month minimum engagement, and monthly packages carry a three-month term. It protects both sides from a relationship ending before it has had a chance to work.

For professionals

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. Applying, vetting and joining the database are free. A one-time fee applies only if we place you under ACE Match.

How and when am I paid?

Under ACE Micro and ACE Manage, by us, monthly. Under ACE Match, directly by your client on the terms you agree.

What if a client reduces the agreed rate?

We hold clients to the remuneration agreed before the search began. Those who renege do not work with us again.

Can I work with more than one client?

Yes, under ACE Micro. Full-time placements are dedicated to one client by design.
