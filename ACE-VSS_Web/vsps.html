What's in It for You

A database that works
for you, not just
around you.

Joining the ACE-VSS database isn't like signing up to a freelance marketplace. You won't be competing with hundreds of others for the same brief. You'll be matched — deliberately — with clients whose needs, communication style, and working culture genuinely fit yours.

A note on the placement fee

When you're placed with a client through ACE Match Standard, a one-time placement fee applies — separate from your client compensation. It covers the matching, vetting, and coordination ACE-VSS does on your behalf. Your agreed compensation with the client is yours in full. We do not deduct from it.

The ACE Standard

Six qualities we
never negotiate on.

The ACE DNA is not a skills checklist. These are the six qualities every professional in our database must already possess — assessed through our vetting process and expected in every client engagement.

Standards that hold — regardless of time zone, client type, or task complexity. Professional isn't a dress code; it's a commitment.

Clients count on you. Consistently. Without being reminded. We build our reputation on yours.

You read the person behind the brief — and you respond to both. The ability to hold that awareness while still delivering is what separates good from exceptional.

Secure in your capability. Humble enough to keep growing. A combination that makes collaboration thrive and long-term client relationships possible.

You figure things out. You don't wait to be spoonfed. The ability to act with good judgment even when the playbook doesn't have an answer.

Feedback sharpens you. It doesn't shake you. Growth is the goal — for you, for your client, and for the standard ACE-VSS holds.

The Vetting Process

Four stages.
One standard.

Our vetting process is thorough because our clients trust us. Every stage is designed to assess the whole person — not just the portfolio.

Questions covering skills, portfolio, availability, work style, accountability approach, and values alignment.

Your work is assessed across five dimensions. Strong portfolios advance quickly. A watchlist option exists — rejection isn't the only outcome.

A 30-minute structured call scored on our 100-point ACE scorecard. Neurodivergent-aware — we evaluate capability and character, not polished delivery.

Welcome pack, security briefing, and written acknowledgement before any client is introduced.

On our neurodivergent-aware assessment: We understand that not everyone performs at their best in structured interview settings. Our scorecard assesses real capability — including how you communicate, handle ambiguity, and respond to feedback — not how polished your delivery is.

The 30-Day Internal Review

After your first placement, ACE-VSS conducts a structured internal review across five areas. This is not punitive — it's how we ensure the match is working well for both you and your client.
