What ACE-VSS Is

A talent intelligence
platform — not an agency.

ACE-VSS is not a marketplace where you browse and hope. We are a talent intelligence platform that does the work of finding the right match — assessing every professional in our database before they are ever placed in front of a client.

A — Adaptable

Our model flexes around how your business actually operates. Whether you need deliverables, a placed professional, or ongoing management — we build around your reality.

C — Curated

Our database is closed-entry. Every VSP has passed a multi-stage assessment and a 100-point scorecard before they are considered for placement. Not everyone gets in.

E — Ethical

VSPs keep 100% of their agreed compensation. ACE-VSS earns only from client-side fees. Transparent billing. No hidden structures. Ever.

Why We Exist

African talent is
exceptional. It deserves
exceptional placement.

We built ACE-VSS because the talent was already there — it just needed a platform that took both sides seriously.

Most staffing models treat professionals as interchangeable. We don't. The way you communicate, how you handle pressure, what your business actually needs day to day — these shape a match more than a CV ever could.

We built this platform so that African professionals get placed with clients who value their work — and clients get professionals who genuinely fit how they operate.

M

Match-First Methodology

Five layers of compatibility assessment: skills, personality, work style, industry fit, and capacity. Not just the first available person.

F

Fair Pay, Always

VSPs keep 100% of their agreed client compensation. ACE-VSS fees are client-side only — always separated on every invoice, always transparent.

A

African Excellence

Our database is built on Africa-based talent. We are actively curating a database where women are the majority — not a diversity metric, but the standard.

Virtual Support Providers

VSPs — not
freelancers.

The professionals in our database are called Virtual Support Providers — VSPs. The distinction matters. A VSP is vetted, matched, supported, and placed with intention. They're not bidding for gigs on a marketplace. They're matched to your business specifically.

The ACE DNA

Six qualities we
never negotiate on.

These aren't aspirational values. They're the baseline every VSP in our database must demonstrate — assessed at vetting and expected in every engagement.

01

Professionalism

Standards that hold — regardless of time zone, client type, or task complexity. Professional isn't a dress code; it's a commitment.

02

Reliability

Clients count on you. Consistently. Without being reminded. We build our reputation on yours.

03

Empathy

You read the person behind the brief — and you respond to both. The ability to hold that awareness while still delivering is what separates good from exceptional.

04

Confidence with Humility

Secure in your capability. Humble enough to keep growing. A combination that makes collaboration thrive and long-term client relationships possible.

05

Common Sense

You figure things out. You don't wait to be spoonfed. The ability to act with good judgment even when the playbook doesn't have an answer.

06

Coachability

Feedback sharpens you. It doesn't shake you. Growth is the goal — for you, for your client, and for the standard ACE-VSS holds.
