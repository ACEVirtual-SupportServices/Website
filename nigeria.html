Pricing questions

The ones we get asked

What if I need 30 hours, or more than 40?

Fifteen and thirty-hour engagements are available on request, priced the same way. Above 40 hours a month you are usually better served by a placement, which costs less per hour and gives you someone dedicated to your business. We will tell you that rather than sell you a bigger package.

What if I do not use all the hours?

You are buying an outcome, not a timesheet. The hours tell you roughly how much work a package holds, so you know what to expect. If the work consistently runs over or under, we resize the package rather than let you overpay or run short.

Is the scope session fee refundable?

No. The fee is not refunded, but if you engage us within 60 days it comes off your first invoice in full. Either way the scope document is yours to keep and use.

How long does a placement take?

Typically three to four weeks from your scope session to a shortlist, depending on the role.

What if the person is not right?

Tell us within the first 30 days and we replace them at no additional sourcing fee. Under ACE Manage that guarantee stays live for the whole engagement.

Are there any fees you have not listed?

No. What you see here is what you pay, and our fees are charged to you rather than deducted from your professional’s pay.
