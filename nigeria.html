The scope session

Monthly support

ACE Micro

A vetted professional handles a defined set of deliverables each month. No search fee, no management charge. One invoice.

Starter

₦70,000 to ₦105,000 / month

Around 10 hours of work a month. Where the price falls depends on the expertise the work needs.

  • For example: basic admin support, covering calendar, scheduling and file organisation
  • or 8 to 12 social posts, designed, captioned and scheduled
  • or 4 to 6 short-form video edits
  • or two blog posts

Steady

₦130,000 to ₦195,000 / month

Around 20 hours of work a month. Where the price falls depends on the expertise the work needs.

  • For example: ongoing admin support with calendar and inbox oversight
  • or one platform managed end to end, with content calendar, 16 to 20 posts and community replies
  • or 10 to 12 short-form video edits, captioned and scheduled
  • or email marketing set up and a monthly newsletter produced

Embedded

₦260,000 to ₦390,000 / month

Around 40 hours of work a month. Where the price falls depends on the expertise the work needs.

  • For example: a combined role such as admin plus social media
  • or a full content operation across two platforms
  • or email marketing strategy, segmentation and campaign production
  • or an ongoing video pipeline with repurposing across formats

Three-month minimum, invoiced monthly in advance. Need mid-level or senior support? Those engagements are quoted individually after your scope session rather than sold as packages.

Placement

ACE Match

When you need your own dedicated person. We run the search, assess for fit as well as skill, and present matched candidates. You employ them and pay them directly.

Sourcing and matching fee
30%

of the first month’s agreed salary, minimum ₦50,000.

  • Half to begin the search, half when you accept a candidate, typically three to four weeks later
  • Hiring more than one? Additional roles are discounted
  • One free replacement within the first 30 days
  • Nothing further to pay after placement

For example, if the role pays ₦300,000 a month, the sourcing fee is ₦90,000.

Placement, managed

ACE Manage

When you want the person, not the managing. You still pay the sourcing fee to find them. From then on you receive one monthly invoice covering their work and our management.

  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Structured check-ins
  • Issue escalation and mediation
  • Continuity cover
  • A replacement guarantee that never expires

Quoted after your scope session. No percentage, no fixed figures published.
