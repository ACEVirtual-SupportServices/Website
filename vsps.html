The Vetting Process

Four stages.
One standard.

Our vetting process is thorough because our clients trust us. Every stage is designed to assess the whole person, not just the portfolio.

Questions covering skills, portfolio, availability, work style, accountability approach, and values alignment.

Your work is assessed across five dimensions. Strong portfolios advance quickly. A watchlist option exists, rejection isn't the only outcome.

A 30-minute structured call scored on our 100-point ACE scorecard. Neurodivergent-aware, we evaluate capability and character, not polished delivery.

Welcome pack, security briefing, and written acknowledgement before any client is introduced.

On our neurodivergent-aware assessment: We understand that not everyone performs at their best in structured interview settings. Our scorecard assesses real capability, including how you communicate, handle ambiguity, and respond to feedback, not how polished your delivery is.

After You're In

What happens
after activation.

Getting into the database is the first step, not the finish line. Here is exactly what happens next.

01

Database activation

Activation means you have cleared our vetting standard and are now visible to be matched with clients. It is the start of your eligibility, not a placement itself.

02

How matching works

You are matched to client briefs by ACE-VSS. You do not bid or compete on proposals. When a brief fits your profile, we bring it to you.

03

How you're engaged and paid

The way you're paid, and who you answer to, depends on the engagement type.

  • ACE Micro: you work through ACE-VSS and are paid by ACE-VSS.
  • ACE Match: you contract with the client directly and are paid by them directly.
  • ACE Manage: you are paid through ACE-VSS, and you have a manager to escalate to if issues arise.
What We Protect

Standards that hold on your side of the table.

R

Remuneration floors
